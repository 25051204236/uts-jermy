How We Met

Our Love Story

October 2024

Kick-Off

We met in the stands at Camp Nou during El Clásico. Out of a sea of Barça fans, fate seated us side by side. When the last-minute goal went in, we screamed, jumped, and hugged without thinking. After the final whistle, we talked over coffee outside the stadium, and the game of a lifetime had begun.

August 2025

Our First Away Day

We packed a bag for our first trip together: a coastal drive along the Costa Brava, then an away day to Madrid to watch Barça in enemy territory. Surrounded by rival fans, we only had eyes for each other. We knew we had found our teammate for life.

February 2026

The Winning Goal

Back home in Barcelona, beneath the warm glow of the Camp Nou floodlights and surrounded by our closest friends, Thiago Messi Roccuzzo knelt at the center of the pitch and Mavie da Silva Santos said “Yes!” The whole stadium seemed to erupt, as if celebrating the greatest goal ever scored.
